# The step-by-step plan: AI-assisted YouTube channel, £0 budget, UK, fully policy-safe

**Companion to `docs/YOUTUBE_AI_MONETISATION_STUDY.md` — read that first.**
The study's verdict: £2,000/month after tax is possible but is a
top-few-percent outcome that realistically takes 18–36 months. This plan
maximises your odds while staying inside YouTube's rules, and includes
honest review gates so you never sink years into something that isn't
working. Expect 12–18 hours a week. Expect £0 income for months. If that's
unacceptable, stop here — that is a legitimate decision.

---

## Ground rules (from the study — these keep the channel alive)

1. **Long-form first.** 8–12 minute videos. Shorts are only ever adverts
   for your long videos (1M Shorts views ≈ £24 — never the plan itself).
2. **AI is your assistant, not your ghost-writer.** AI drafts; you research,
   fact-check, restructure, and rewrite in your own voice. Every video must
   contain your own angle. This is the difference between "monetisable" and
   "demonetised" under the July 2025 inauthentic-content policy.
3. **One format, repeated.** One niche, one recognisable style, so viewers
   (and the algorithm) know what your channel is.
4. **Never-do list** (each of these can kill the channel): reading text you
   didn't write; misleading titles/thumbnails; movie/TV/music clips
   ("fair use" will not save you — 3 copyright strikes deletes the
   channel); deepfakes or cloned voices of real people; medical or election
   claims; buying subs/views; free-tier AI music (Suno/Udio) or free-tier
   ElevenLabs voiceover in monetised videos; mislabelling kids' content.
5. **Disclose synthetic content.** Tick "Altered content: Yes" at upload
   whenever anything realistic-looking is AI-made. It costs nothing.

## Phase 0 — Decisions and setup (week 1, ~4 hours)

**Step 1. Lock the niche: finance/business storytelling.**
Stories about companies, frauds, collapses, rises and falls — told like
mini-documentaries. NOT investment advice, ever (policy scrutiny + UK
regulatory risk). Why this niche: highest revenue per view on YouTube (so
the lowest views target), proven faceless by UK creators, and the moat is
research/writing — which costs time, not money.
*(If it truly doesn't interest you, the runner-up is disaster/mystery
narration. Pick one. Do not run two channels.)*

**Step 2. Create the channel properly.**
1. Create a fresh Google account for the channel (keeps business separate).
2. Turn on 2-Step Verification (Google Account → Security). Required for
   monetisation later.
3. Create the YouTube channel: pick a name that describes the format, not
   you (check it's free on YouTube search first).
4. In YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility: enable
   Advanced Features (phone verification). This unlocks thumbnail A/B
   testing later.

**Step 3. Install the £0 toolkit.**
- Editing: **CapCut** (easiest) or **Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11) —
  both export 1080p with no watermark on free.
- Thumbnails: **Canva** free account + **ideogram.ai** free account
  (10 images/day, commercial-safe).
- Footage: bookmark **pexels.com** and **pixabay.com** (both verified free
  for monetised videos, no credit needed).
- Music: only ever from **YouTube Studio → Audio Library**.
- Research/SEO: **vidiq.com** free account.
- Script drafting: the AI chat you already use (Claude).
- Voiceover: **your own voice** into your phone or laptop mic in a quiet
  room (free, and the strongest protection against the "inauthentic
  content" policy). If you genuinely can't narrate, use **Kokoro TTS**
  (free, open-source, commercially safe — search "Kokoro TTS web demo");
  budget for ElevenLabs Starter (~£4/month) as the first-ever paid upgrade.
- AI video accents (optional, always disclosed): **flow.google** (50 free
  credits/day ≈ 5 short Veo clips, watermarked) and **create.wan.video**
  (10 credits/day + slow unlimited queue).

**Step 4. Study the winners (this is work, not procrastination).**
Watch 10 videos each from MagnatesMedia, How Money Works, and Economics
Explained. For every video write one line: what was the hook in the first
30 seconds, how was the story structured, what did the title/thumbnail
promise? You are learning the format you'll compete in.

## Phase 1 — The first ten videos (months 1–3)

**The goal is NOT views or money. It is: learn the craft, find your angle,
build the habit.** Views in this phase are usually tens, not thousands.
That is normal and expected — do not judge the project yet.

**Step 5. Build a topic bank (2 hours, refill monthly).**
Ask the AI for 50 business-story ideas in categories: famous frauds,
companies that died, unlikely fortunes, business rivalries, "how X really
makes money". For each, check on YouTube: are there successful videos on
this topic (demand exists) where the best version is old, thin, or badly
made (room for you)? Shortlist 15.

**Step 6. The per-video production loop (~10–15 hours at first, target
6–9 by video ten).**
1. **Research (2–3h):** gather the story from primary sources (news
   archives, court documents, company filings). Note every fact with its
   source. Ask the AI to help find angles, but verify every number, date
   and name yourself — one confidently wrong fact in a finance video
   destroys credibility and invites misinformation flags.
2. **Script (2–3h):** draft with AI *from your research notes*, then
   rewrite it in your own words, in your own structure, with your own take.
   Target ~1,300–1,600 words for 9–11 minutes. First 30 seconds: state the
   most surprising thing in the story and why it matters. Short sentences.
3. **Voiceover (30–60m):** record in a quiet room, standing, slightly
   slower than feels natural. Re-record fluffs; don't chase perfection.
4. **Edit (3–5h):** narration on the timeline; change the visual every 3–6
   seconds (stock clip, AI still with slow zoom, simple text, map);
   Audio Library music low under the voice; export 1080p.
5. **Package (1h):** thumbnail in Canva/Ideogram — 3–5 words max, one
   strong image, high contrast. Write 5 titles, pick the one that creates
   the biggest honest curiosity gap. Never promise what the video doesn't
   deliver.
6. **Upload:** paste your script into Studio's caption tool (auto-syncs to
   near-perfect captions); description + tags via vidIQ free; set
   "not made for kids"; tick "Altered content" if any realistic AI visuals;
   schedule.

**Step 7. Cadence: one video per week, every week.** Ten videos by the end
of month 3. Consistency beats volume — and mass-production is literally
against the monetisation policy.

**⛔ GATE 1 (end of month 3, after 10 videos):** Are you still willing to do
this weekly? Is average view duration above ~35–40% on recent videos?
Is *any* video finding an audience (a few hundred organic views)? If all
answers are no and the work feels unbearable — stop here, having spent £0.
If retention is decent but views are low, continue: that's the normal
shape at this stage.

## Phase 2 — Iterate toward monetisation (months 4–9)

**Step 8. Double down on what the data says.** In YouTube Studio, for each
video check: click-through rate (CTR), average view duration, and where
people quit. Make more of what retained; kill formats that didn't. Study
your best video's comments for what viewers actually cared about.

**Step 9. Sharpen packaging.** Once videos get ~1,000+ impressions, use
Test & Compare (Studio's built-in thumbnail A/B test — available with
Advanced Features, no monetisation needed) on every upload.

**Step 10. Add a Shorts funnel (only now).** 2–3 Shorts a week cut from
your long videos' best 45 seconds, each pointing to the full video. Their
job is discovery, not revenue.

**Step 11. Apply for monetisation the moment you qualify.**
Thresholds: **1,000 subscribers AND 4,000 public watch hours in 12
months**. In Studio → Earn, apply. You'll create an AdSense account —
immediately do the tax form:
- AdSense → Payments → Payments info → Settings → Manage settings →
  United States tax info → **W-8BEN**, claim **US–UK treaty benefits**
  (0% withholding; your National Insurance number works as the foreign
  TIN). Without this Google can withhold 24% of everything.
- Diary a reminder to redo the W-8BEN every 3 years.
If rejected at review: read the stated reason, fix exactly that (usually
"reused content" — add more visible original analysis, more of your own
voice), re-apply after 30 days. This is common and recoverable.

**Step 12. UK tax admin (15 minutes, once).** In whatever tax year your
*gross* YouTube income first passes £1,000: register for Self Assessment
at gov.uk ("register for Self Assessment", sole trader) by the following
5 October. Keep AdSense statements and any receipts. When your first
January tax bill eventually comes, it will include 50% on account for the
next year — save ~30% of YouTube income from the start and it will never
hurt you.

**⛔ GATE 2 (month 9):** Monetised, or clearly on track (500+ subs, watch
hours accumulating, retention >40%)? If yes, continue. If the channel is
flat at near-zero after ~35 videos, the honest reading is that this format
isn't finding a market: either change the format substantially (one pivot,
based on your analytics) or stop. Do not restart the same thing and hope.

## Phase 3 — From monetised to meaningful money (months 9–24+)

**Step 13. First £100–£500/month (AdSense).** At this stage income follows
views mechanically. Reinvest the first income in order: ElevenLabs Starter
(~£4/mo) if using TTS; a decent USB microphone (~£60–£100) if narrating;
Google AI Pro (£18.99/mo) only when AI clips are genuinely part of your
style.

**Step 14. Open the second income stream at ~10k subscribers.** Put an
affiliate link relevant to each video in the description (Amazon
Associates UK or a finance-adjacent programme — always with the required
disclosure). Expect £50–£500/month at moderate scale.

**Step 15. Sponsorships at ~25k–50k subscribers (this is what closes the
gap).** Brand deals are ~half of creator income economy-wide. List
yourself on sponsor marketplaces, answer inbound seriously, charge
properly (a 60-second integration at 50k+ engaged subs is £200–£1,000+).
One to two integrations a month plus AdSense plus affiliate at ~350k
monthly views is the realistic £2,335/month configuration — versus
~630k+ views on ads alone.

**Step 16. Protect the asset.** Never chase a trend outside your lane;
never buy engagement; keep fact-checking discipline as you scale; keep
ticking the disclosure box; re-read YouTube's monetisation policy page
twice a year (it moved in 2025 and will move again).

**⛔ GATE 3 (month 18–24):** £500+/month and growing → this is now a real
business; keep compounding. £100–£500 and growing → continue, revisit in 6
months. Below £100/month at month 24 → the market has answered; wind down
or accept it as a hobby.

## What success actually looks like (so expectations stay honest)

| Milestone | Realistic timing if it's working |
|---|---|
| First 1,000 organic views on one video | Months 2–6 |
| 1,000 subscribers | Months 8–15 (typical for channels that make it) |
| Monetisation approved | Months 9–16 |
| First £100 month | Months 10–18 |
| First £1,000 month | Months 15–30 |
| **£2,335/month pre-tax (= £2,000 net)** | **Months 18–36, top-few-percent outcome** |

Any given month, the most likely outcome remains "not yet". The gates
exist so that "not yet" never silently becomes "never" without a decision.

## The one-page compliance checklist

- [ ] 2FA on; Advanced Features enabled; "not made for kids" on every upload
- [ ] Every script: my research, my structure, my words (AI assisted only)
- [ ] Every fact checked against a primary source before recording
- [ ] Visuals only from Pexels/Pixabay/my own AI generations; music only
      from YouTube Audio Library; no movie/TV/news clips, ever
- [ ] "Altered content: Yes" ticked when any realistic visual is synthetic
- [ ] No free-tier ElevenLabs/Suno/Kling/etc. output in any monetised video
      (their free tiers are non-commercial)
- [ ] Titles/thumbnails promise only what the video delivers
- [ ] W-8BEN filed in AdSense (and renewed every 3 years)
- [ ] Self Assessment registered once gross income passed £1,000/yr;
      ~30% of income saved for tax
