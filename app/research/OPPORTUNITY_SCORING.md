## Scoring method

Six dimensions from the mission brief, 1–10 each, equal-weighted composite
(×100/60). Scores are judgements grounded in the cited evidence — treat ±5
composite points as noise. **T** flags a timing discount (revenue >12 months
away or rules not final). Constraints already applied as hard filters
(from verified research): buyer must have no procurement function; product
must be async; duty must recur; prospects must be enumerable; no free
government tool or platform-subsidised incumbent doing the same job.

## The graveyard (excluded before scoring, with the killing evidence)

- **Taxpayer-facing MTD filing** — HMRC free tool + five banks bundle MTD
  software free to win current accounts; price floor is £0
- **Invoicing/bookkeeping** — QuickBooks £10, FreeAgent free-with-bank-account
- **Electrical/gas certificate apps** — ≥5 UK vendors at £11–26/mo
- **Salon/beauty booking** — Fresha payments-subsidised at £14.95
- **Trades job management** — 9+ vendors at £15–44/user
- **UK e-invoicing** — mandate is 2029, outside window
- **Companies House micro-filing** — free government tool confirmed
- **Food safety/SFBB digital** — saturated with free tiers; allergen rules
  are guidance, not law
- **Driving instructor tools** — free incumbent (ADI Network)
- **Generic CRM/project management** — free tiers everywhere
- **Shopify apps as launch channel** — median new-app revenue $0
- **Anything needing SOC 2, HMRC recognition, or FCA authorisation in yr 1**

## Finalists sent to competitor deep-dive (Stage 2b, running)

1. **Landlord/agent compliance register** — front-runner. Checks commissioned:
   full competitor price ladder, new RRA-specific entrants, whether the
   government database itself will do reminders/storage (product-shrinking
   risk), NRLA member tools, regulatory ground truth + timing, pricing model.
2. **MTD client-status board** — runner-up/fallback. Checks: checkmymtd
   maturity, HMRC agent-dashboard risk, practice-tool pricing, reachability.
3. **Revenue-recovery automation** — the owner's hypothesis, given a fair
   adversarial trial rather than a desk rejection.

Decision rule: front-runner survives unless (a) the government portal itself
ships reminders/certificate storage, (b) ≥3 credible funded entrants already
own "RRA compliance", or (c) regulatory timing slips beyond mid-2027 AND
certificate-tracking alone can't carry the sale. If killed → runner-up.
