# R3 Validation: Martyn's Law Enhanced-Tier Compliance System (£99-149/mo) — KILL

## 1. The decisive number: ~24,300 enhanced-tier premises, and the wrong kind of owner

The Home Office impact assessment for the final Act puts **178,900 premises in scope: ~154,600 standard tier and ~24,300 enhanced tier** ([gov.uk IA / search extracts](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/terrorism-protection-of-premises-bill-2024-impact-assessment), [legislation.gov.uk impacts page](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/10/impacts), corroborated by the [2023 IA PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/645128452f62220013a6a3d6/2023-04-25_Martyn_s_Law_Impact_Assessment.pdf) which used the same 24,300 enhanced figure).

24,300 sounds like a market until you look at who owns an 800+ capacity building. The enhanced tier is: stadiums, arenas, shopping centres, universities, large hospitals, exhibition/conference centres, transport hubs, large hotels, big theatres and nightclubs ([Diamond Systems](https://diamondsystems.co.uk/2025/04/23/who-will-need-to-comply-with-martyns-law/), [United Outcomes](https://united-outcomes.com/explaining-enhanced-duty-tier-premises-under-martyns-law/), [SJA](https://shop.sja.org.uk/pages/understanding-martyns-law-standard-vs-enhanced-tier)). These are NHS trusts, universities, councils, REIT/FM-managed shopping centres, hotel chains, theatre groups (ATG), venue operators (AMG, Live Nation), and club groups — i.e. **organisations with procurement functions, existing H&S/security teams, and incumbent platforms**. This is exactly the SOC-2 wall. NHS England has already issued its own sector application guidance ([NHS England, Nov 2025](https://www.england.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PRN02056-netb-terrorism-protection-of-premises-act-2025-application-to-healthcare-buildings.pdf)) — the public-sector slice buys through G-Cloud, not from an unknown solo vendor. The genuinely independent 800+ venue (an independent large club, events hall, big church-adjacent venue — noting **all places of worship were placed in the standard tier regardless of size**, removing cathedrals from the pool) is a thin residue of the 24,300 — plausibly low single-digit thousands — and it is the most price-sensitive, consultant-preferring slice. The IA's own regulatory scrutiny (RPC opinion) flagged that small/micro-business impact assessment was weak precisely because the enhanced tier is dominated by larger operators ([RPC opinion](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/645100812f6222000ca6a39c/2023-03-15-RPC-HO-5254_1__Martyns_Law__Protect_Duty___002_.pdf)).

Price-anchor problem inside the number: the government's own estimate of the **entire annual enhanced-tier compliance cost is ~£5,210/year, "mainly reflecting staff time"** ([Home Office myth buster via Astutis/imabi](https://www.imabi.com/blog/martyns-law-home-office-myth-buster-guidance); 10-year PV £52,093/premises per the IA). At £99-149/mo (£1,188-1,788/yr) the product would claim 23-34% of the total compliance envelope the government has told buyers to expect. That is a visibly mispriced ask.

## 2. Swarm check: the swarm has already landed, including an exact clone

Prior research saw template sellers. As of Aug 2026 there are **dedicated SaaS systems**, and one is a near-verbatim copy of concept R3:

- **[martynslawsoftware.co.uk](https://martynslawsoftware.co.uk/)** — "audit-ready records, procedure templates, staff training… drill logs with timestamped photos… export a single PDF evidence pack." That is R3's feature list, live today, at **£29/month** (standard tier, up to 10 venues centrally managed, enhanced-tier support available, annual 20% discount, no setup fees) ([pricing page](https://martynslawsoftware.co.uk/pricing)).
- **[Serious Incident Manager — "SIM Martyn's Law Manager"](https://seriousincidentmanager.com/martyns-law-manager/)** — dedicated Martyn's Law module, **listed on UK Government G-Cloud Digital Marketplace** ([listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/342763487779972)), i.e. already plumbed into the public-sector procurement channel that owns much of the enhanced tier.
- **[Noted](https://www.notedhq.com/industries/public-venue-safety-martyns-law)** — venue-safety compliance app: drills, briefings, training logs, inspection records for inspectors/insurers.
- **[D4H](https://www.d4h.com/blog/how-d4h-supports-compliance-with-martyns-law)** — established emergency-management platform marketing Martyn's Law compliance.
- **[Halo Solutions](https://www.halosolutions.com/the-black-box-mandate/)** — the incumbent UK event/stadium safety command platform, positioning hard on Martyn's Law; the natural home for exactly the enhanced-tier venues R3 targets.
- **[Handy AV's MAVAS / martynslawsolutions.com](https://www.avinteractive.com/territories-news/uki/handy-av-launches-martyns-law-consultancy-platform-22-01-2026/)** (launched Jan 2026), plus access-control incumbents bundling Martyn's Law messaging: [Paxton Net2](https://www.paxton-access.com/supporting-martyns-law-with-net2/), [Controlsoft](https://www.controlsoft.com/martyns-law/), [dormakaba](https://www.dormakaba.com/gb-en/news/martyns-law), [Algo](https://www.algosolutions.com/resources/regulatory-compliance/martyns-law/), [COREDINATE](https://coredinate.de/en/blog/event-security-martyns-law-2026).
- Template/consultancy layer thickened: [Policy Pros sells the enhanced-tier compliance document itself](https://www.policypros.co.uk/martyns-law-enhanced-tier-compliance-document/); consultancies ([1705](https://1705consultancy.co.uk/martyns-law/), [Who Dares Group](https://whodaresgroup.co.uk/martyns-law-consultancy/), [Martyn's Law Assessments](https://martynslawassessments.co.uk/), [Ubique](https://ubiqueriskmanagement.co.uk/news-insights/what-is-martyns-law/)) sell end-to-end packages.

This is the identical commoditisation pattern that killed the previous three concepts, ~8 months before enforcement even begins.

## 3. Timing ground truth (Aug 2026)

- Royal Assent 3 April 2025; **enforcement/commencement expected Spring 2027** ([Ops Con](https://theopscon.com/intelligence/martyns-law-compliance-clock-summer-2026-07-05), [LexisNexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/news/sia-publishes-update-on-martyn-s-law-regulatory-preparations)).
- Home Office **section 27 statutory guidance published 15 April 2026** ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-terrorism-protection-of-premises-act-2025/terrorism-protection-of-premises-act-2025-statutory-guidance)) — so requirement detail exists.
- BUT the **SIA's section 12 regulator guidance (how it will inspect/enforce — i.e. what "SIA-ready" actually means) was consulted on 15 April–12 June 2026 and final guidance lands only in autumn 2026** ([gov.uk SIA consultation](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/sia-launches-consultation-on-section-12-guidance-for-martyns-law), [Ops Con](https://theopscon.com/intelligence/martyns-law-commencement-no2-sia-duties-june-2026)). "SIA-ready export" would be built against a spec that is still moving, and the entire vendor field re-converges on the final guidance the moment it publishes.

## 4. Standard-tier reconsideration (£19-29/mo mass product): worse, not better

154,600 premises, but: the final Act imposes **no documentation requirement on standard tier** — procedures only, "reasonably practicable", with the Home Office's official **Myth Buster explicitly stating "there is no requirement to buy specialist services or products… official guidance will be self-explanatory and freely available"** and estimating standard-tier cost at ~£330/yr of staff time ([Bira on the Myth Buster](https://bira.co.uk/resources/the-home-office-s-myth-buster-guide-on-martyn-s-law-how-small-businesses-can-prepare/), [imabi](https://www.imabi.com/blog/martyns-law-home-office-myth-buster-guidance), [Fieldfisher 10 myths](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/martyn-s-law-10-myths-about-the-terrorism-protection-of-premises-act-2025)). The government is running an active anti-purchase campaign: free [ProtectUK hub](https://www.protectuk.police.uk/martyns-law), **free ACT Awareness e-learning as the recognised training baseline**, free templates from multiple parties ([Camelott](https://camelott.co.uk/martyns-law-protection-plan/), [First Aid Training Cooperative terrorism risk assessment](https://firstaidtrainingcooperative.co.uk/terrorism-risk-assessment/), [rradar checklist](https://rradar.com/martyns-law-checklist/)). And SIM already occupies the £29/mo slot. This fails the founder's own criterion twice over: free government/association tooling does the job, and the category is keyword-commoditised.

## 5. Buyer reality for the independent enhanced venue

The task lands on the **Designated Senior Individual — by law a director/partner-level person personally accountable** ([KGFM](https://kgfm.co.uk/martyns-law), [Kennedys](https://www.kennedyslaw.com/en/thought-leadership/article/2025/terrorism-protection-of-premises-act-2025-an-overview-of-the-standard-and-enhanced-duties/)). Enhanced-tier documents must be **submitted to the SIA**, with sanctions for enhanced breaches up to £18m/5% of worldwide revenue widely reported ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2025/05/terrorism-protection-of-premises-act-2025--new-measures-for-enhanced-safety)). A personally-liable director facing a terrorism regulator does not de-risk with an unknown solo founder's £99 web app: they buy a consultant's assessment + document (the fire-risk-assessment purchasing pattern the founder already knows — one-off expert sign-off, not software), or a platform with insurance-grade credibility (Halo, D4H) or G-Cloud standing (SIM). Here the "liability-grade audit trail" moat inverts: **liability-grade is precisely what a no-track-record vendor cannot credibly sell in this category**. Consultancies are also giving the ongoing-records layer away as retention glue.

## 6. SafetyCulture/iAuditor absorption

No dedicated Martyn's Law template confirmed in SafetyCulture's library as of Aug 2026, but its free tier plus generic **venue security inspection templates** ([venue inspection checklist](https://safetyculture.com/library/hospitality/venue-inspection-v11)) and a 100k-template library absorb the drills/inspections/logs component for free the day anyone bothers to publish one — a when, not if, given the SafetyDocs template mill. The differentiated remainder (the compliance document itself) is exactly what Policy Pros sells as a one-off and consultants bundle.

## Why this kills, in one line each
1. **SOC-2 wall confirmed**: ~24,300 enhanced premises are overwhelmingly procurement-bearing organisations; the reachable independent slice is a few thousand price-sensitive venues at most.
2. **Exact clone exists at £29/mo** (martynslawsoftware.co.uk) plus a G-Cloud incumbent, an events-sector incumbent (Halo), and a Jan-2026 platform launch — the swarm arrived before enforcement.
3. **The government is the anti-vendor**: official Myth Buster says buy nothing; guidance, training and templates are free.
4. **Trust asymmetry**: personally-liable DSIs submitting documents to a terrorism regulator under £18m penalties will not anchor on an unknown solo product.
5. **Spec still moving**: SIA's definitive enforcement guidance lands autumn 2026, resetting every vendor to the same start line with better distribution than a solo founder.

## Verdict

KILL — the enhanced tier's ~24,300 premises are overwhelmingly procurement-function organisations (the SOC-2 wall), and the thin independent remainder is already served by an exact £29/month clone of this concept plus G-Cloud and event-safety incumbents, in a category where the government's own Myth Buster tells buyers they need to purchase nothing.
