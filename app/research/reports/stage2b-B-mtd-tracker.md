# Concept B kill-review: MTD client-status board for small UK practices

## 1. Competitive field — the concept's core job is already a headline feature, twice over

**Free, platform-subsidised dashboards (violates a verified constraint).** Xero HQ — free to partner firms — already shows "every client's ITSA status, deadlines, alerts, and submission history in one screen," with Tax Manager filterable by tax type/status and a bulk quarterly submission tool shipped in 2025 (https://www.xero.com/uk/programme/making-tax-digital/mtd-itsa-quarterly-updates/, https://mtd.digital/xero-mtd/). QuickBooks Online Accountant (free) shows per-quarter due alerts and green-tick completion per client (https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-uk/help-article/income-taxes/send-quarterly-update-mtd-income-tax-quickbooks/L5NAvTc5I_GB_en_GB). FreeAgent gives partners a free Practice Dashboard managing all clients' MTD ITSA filings end-to-end, and the client licences are free with NatWest/RBS/Mettle banking (https://www.freeagent.com/accountants/practice-dashboard/, https://www.natwest.com/business/business-services/freeagent/accountant.html). Sage announced an "MTD AI Agent" for accountants in Sept 2025 (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250923241131/en/). These only cover their own-ledger clients — but that free coverage shrinks the paid problem to "clients scattered across software," and that remainder is covered by:

**Cheap small-practice PM/tax suites that track MTD across any client software.** BrightManager (ex-AccountancyManager, the default PM tool of exactly this segment) is £31.20–£33.60+VAT/user/month and "automatically generates quarterly tasks as deadlines approach, tracks progress, and maintains oversight of all MTD obligations across your practice" — plus its signature automated client-chasing emails (https://brightsg.com/blog/new-brightmanager-mtd-features-transform-practice-management/, https://taxdome.com/blog/brightmanager). For a sole practitioner that is ~£34/month for a full PM suite including the MTD board — undercutting the proposed £49–99 for tracking alone. IRIS Elements/Senta runs £25–32/user/month (https://curatesuite.com/accounting/tools/senta); Glide £82.46+VAT/month first user (https://contentsnare.com/accounting-practice-management-software-uk/); Capium is positioned even cheaper for solo firms (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/reviews/practice-management-software/capium).

**The devastating price anchor: TaxCalc.** TaxCalc — ubiquitous in sole-practitioner tax work — sells MTD Quarterly Filer, practice version, from **£112/YEAR**, managing quarterly updates for multiple clients who stay on spreadsheets, and its Communications Centre auto-generates info-request emails with recurring reminders until marked received (https://www.taxcalc.com/mtdQuarterlyFiler, https://www.taxcalc.com/solutions/making-tax-digital). That is filing + chasing for ~£9/month against the concept's £49–99/month for tracking without filing.

**Micro-competitors already carpet-bombing the niche.** mtd.tax "MTD Companion for Agents" (https://mtd.tax/agents.html), Streem Connect multi-client agent dashboards (https://streemconnect.com/mtd-software/), GoFile's agent product (https://gofile.co.uk/income-tax-mtd-for-accountants/), filemtditsa.co.uk with a firm-registration flow (https://www.filemtditsa.co.uk/pricing), aligned.tax free Excel MTD software (https://aligned.tax/), plus listicles of "top 10 FREE MTD software for accountants" (https://figsflow.com/8-free-hmrc-approved-mtd-software-for-accountants-and-tax-agents/). checkmymtd.co.uk itself appears to be a free 20-second mandation-date calculator with guides — lead-gen grade, not an entrenched paid product (https://checkmymtd.co.uk/guides/mtd-income-guide); direct fetch was proxy-blocked so its backer/pricing is unverified.

## 2. HMRC agent dashboard — no kill-shot from HMRC, but a trap on the other flank

HMRC's Agent Services Account deliberately shows **no client list** — ICAEW documents this as a security design decision (https://www.icaew.com/technical/tax/working-with-hmrc/agent-services-account), and no agent-facing MTD status dashboard appears in coverage of the HMRC Transformation Roadmap 2026 update (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/hmrc-transformation-roadmap-progress-update-2026/hmrc-transformation-roadmap-update-2026 — primary doc proxy-blocked, read via extracts). The "confirmation gap" is real and freshly documented: AccountingWEB's "Receipt or deceit? The MTD confirmation gap" (Aug 2026, Robyn Milstead) confirms there is no standard HMRC acknowledgement and the only independent check is logging into the Gateway per client (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/tax/hmrc-policy/receipt-or-deceit-the-mtd-confirmation-gap).

But here is the trap: to show real HMRC obligation status (fulfilled/open per client) rather than hand-keyed status, the product must call HMRC's MTD ITSA obligations API — which requires HMRC developer onboarding, production credentials, fraud-prevention headers and agent-authorisation flows (https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/guides/income-tax-mtd-end-to-end-service-guide/). That is precisely the HMRC-approval burden the concept was framed to avoid. Without it, the product is a shared spreadsheet with reminder emails; with it, the "needs NO HMRC recognition" premise is false.

## 3. Demand evidence — real pain, but pain every incumbent is monetising right now

The first quarterly deadline (7 Aug 2026) was chaos: failed submissions, conflicting records, access problems, last-minute client scrambles (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/practice/general-practice/how-mtd-reached-its-first-deadline-in-chaos; also https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/one-week-until-the-first-quarterly-update-deadline — full threads proxy-blocked). Roughly 864,000 taxpayers mandated from April 2026, ~1.08m more in 2027, ~975k in 2028 (https://www.icaew.com/insights/tax-news/2025/aug-2025/hmrc-offers-agents-support-with-mtd-for-income-tax). The pain is genuine and recurring — which is exactly why Xero, Intuit, Sage, IRIS, Bright and TaxCalc all shipped or headline this feature in 2025–26. An August-2026 pain point with billion-pound vendors already deployed is not an open gap; it is the most contested square on the board.

## 4. Reachability — this dimension actually passes

65–70% of UK practices are sole practitioners or two-partner firms (https://apma.co.uk/buying-into-an-accountancy-practice/); they are enumerable via ICAEW/ACCA/AAT directories, mostly incorporated (cold-emailable as corporate subscribers under PECR), congregate on AccountingWEB, and attend Accountex (300+ exhibitors — which also demonstrates how crowded the vendor field is: https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/technical-activities/uk-tech/in-practice-ezine-archive/In-Practice-archive-2025/February/First-look-at-Accountex-London-2025.html). Reaching 24–48 practices is feasible. Reachability is not the problem.

## 5. Willingness to pay — the anchors are brutal

The segment's revealed price points for adjacent tools: TaxCalc MTD practice filing from £112/year; BrightManager full PM ~£33.60+VAT/user/month; Senta £25–32/user/month; sole-trader MTD filing software ~£30/month is already described as expensive in the market (https://creative.accountants/business-tax/mtd-quarterly-reporting/). AccountingWEB's "MTD Pricing" discussions are about how to pass MTD costs to clients — i.e., practices treat every new MTD pound as a cost to recover, not absorb (https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/any-answers/mtd-pricing-0). A £99/month tracking-only tool must survive four rebuttals in every sales conversation: "Xero HQ/QBOA is free," "BrightManager already does this in my PM suite," "TaxCalc is £112 a year," and "my spreadsheet works." At £49 it is still 5x the TaxCalc anchor for less function.

## Failure summary

- Constraint violated: platform-subsidised incumbents (free Xero HQ, QBOA, FreeAgent partner dashboards) cover the job for single-ledger practices.
- Multi-ledger remainder covered by BrightManager/TaxCalc/Capium at a fraction of the proposed price, with automated chasing included.
- The only real differentiator (HMRC's own obligation status, cross-software, closing the confirmation gap) requires HMRC API onboarding — contradicting the concept's no-HMRC-approval premise; without it the product is manual-entry.
- HMRC could close the confirmation gap with one ASA release at any time; the documented gap is the product's whole moat.
- Willingness to pay anchored at £9–34/month equivalents by tools practices already own.

VERDICT: KILL — the job is already covered at near-zero marginal cost by tools small practices own (free Xero HQ/QBOA/FreeAgent partner dashboards for single-ledger clients; BrightManager at ~£34/user/month and TaxCalc from £112/year with automated chasing for the cross-software case), and the concept's only defensible differentiator — showing HMRC's own obligation status across software to close the documented confirmation gap — requires exactly the HMRC API onboarding the concept was premised on avoiding; without it the £49–99/month product is a manually-updated spreadsheet with reminders priced 5–10x above the segment's anchors.

GAPS: Proxy blocked direct fetches of checkmymtd.co.uk, mtd.tax, accountingweb.co.uk, gov.uk and taxcalc.com, so: (1) checkmymtd's backer, maturity and any paid tier are unverified beyond search extracts; (2) MTD Companion for Agents' pricing/feature depth is unknown; (3) full AccountingWEB thread content ('we track it in Excel' sentiment) is inferred from headlines and extracts, not read verbatim; (4) the HMRC Transformation Roadmap 2026 update was not read in primary form — absence of an announced agent dashboard rests on secondary coverage; (5) Accountex exhibitor stand costs and AccountingWEB advertising rates were not found; (6) Karbon UK per-user pricing was not captured this session (market context suggests it sits above BrightManager); (7) Coconut accountant-portal pricing not found. None of these gaps plausibly reverses the verdict — they mostly concern how weak the micro-competitors are, and the verdict rests on the strong incumbents whose pricing was verified.
