VERDICT: confirmed
NOTE: Independently confirmed by ICAS, rossmartin.co.uk and FreeAgent: no penalty points for late quarterly updates in 2026-27 (confirmed at Autumn Budget 2025); points-based penalties apply from 6 April 2027 to all self-assessment taxpayers, not just MTD; £200 at four points for quarterly filers. A real forcing function therefore already exists in 2026/27 — it just crystallises at the January 2028 return deadline rather than at each quarter. That is a purchase trigger inside the window, not outside it.
