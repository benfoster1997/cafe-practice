# Pricing & Packaging: UK Landlord/Agent Compliance Product

**Method note / evidence quality:** every vendor pricing domain (landlordvision.co.uk, landlordstudio.com, usehammock.com, lendlord.io, alphaletz.com, arthuronline.co.uk, coho.life, plus the four compliance-only micro-sites) was blocked by the network egress proxy, so no page was fetched directly. All prices below come from search-result extracts of those vendor pages plus third-party review/comparison sites. Several sources (RentalBux, Latch/uselatch, LettingGuru, landlord-os, letavo, LetCompliance) are competitor content-marketing blogs — usable for competitor prices but with incentive to distort. Confidence per figure is flagged.

## 2. The compliance-only sub-market is already swarmed — and priced at £0-19/mo

This is the single most damaging finding. Search surfaced at least seven live UK compliance-tracker micro-products doing exactly the certificate-expiry job:

- **Landlord HQ** — flat **£4.99/mo or £49/YEAR**, all properties, no per-property fees (https://www.landlordhq.uk/). That is one year of the whole product for less than one month at the proposed £49.
- **LetCompliance** — free for 1 property; Starter £14.99/mo (≤2), Pro £19/mo (≤10), **Unlimited £49/mo** (https://letcompliance.com/pricing). £49/mo is the *top* of this niche, for unlimited properties.
- **landlordcompliancecheck.co.uk** — free forever for 1 property, 60-day pre-expiry emails; £9/mo Pro planned (https://landlordcompliancecheck.co.uk/track).
- **LandlordRiskCheck** — free, no account required, tracks gas/EICR/EPC with 60-180 day warnings (https://landlordriskcheck.co.uk/app-dashboard.html).
- **landlordcompliance.io**, **ukcompliancereminders.co.uk**, **managelet.co.uk** — same job; prices not extractable (sites proxy-blocked) but positioned as "simple monthly service".
- **Latch** — free tier includes a compliance calendar for ≤3 leases; Pro £20/mo (https://www.uselatch.co.uk/blog/free-landlord-software-uk-2026-comparison — competitor-authored, low-medium confidence).

## 6. The £0 competitor check (answering point 6 directly)

Yes — certificate tracking is effectively already free for small portfolios, from multiple directions:
- **Landlord Studio GO** (free ≤3 units) includes automated reminders for safety inspections, certificate renewals, rent reviews, tenancy end dates (https://www.landlordstudio.com/uk/features/landlord-compliance; caveat: one third-party source claims compliance alerts sit behind paid tiers on accounting-focused tools — unresolved conflict).
- **Alphaletz free** (≤3 properties) includes automated task reminder emails and document storage.
- **Lendlord free** core includes compliance tracking and has shipped a free Renters' Rights Act compliance tool in 2026 (https://www.propertyreporter.co.uk/lendlord-launches-tool-to-help-landlords-prove-renters-rights-act-compliance.html).
- **NRLA Portfolio**: free automated compliance reminders bundled into the £125/yr membership that compliance-anxious landlords disproportionately already hold. This is the closest thing to a "platform-subsidised incumbent" in this niche and arguably violates the prior-research constraint.
- Pure-free standalones: LandlordRiskCheck (free, no account), landlordcompliancecheck (free 1 property), LetCompliance (free 1 property), Rentila (free 2 properties).

## 2b. What portfolio landlords actually pay (stack evidence)

No direct forum/survey data was recoverable (two searches returned SEO content, not Property118/PropertyTribes threads — flagged as a gap). Inference from the ladder: a 10-property landlord using paid software pays **£15-35/mo** (Hammock £15+VAT, Landlord Vision £34.97, Alphaletz ~£10-17); a 25-property landlord pays **£30-100/mo** (Hammock £25+VAT band, LV Enterprise £99.97, COHO £62.50). Third-party synthesis puts typical UK landlord spend at ~£6-20/mo entry level (https://www.uselatch.co.uk/blog/free-landlord-software-uk-2026-comparison; https://intercom.help/august-fdb1e1d54834/en/articles/13976193-the-landlord-s-guide-to-property-management-software-costs-in-2026). A compliance-only line item of £49-99/mo would exceed most target customers' entire current software stack.

## 3. Micro letting agents

- **10Ninety**: £70/mo up to 100 managed lettings (+£35 per extra 100, £100 setup) (https://www.10ninety.co.uk/ via https://portalhub.co.uk/blogstory.php?bid=64).
- Market synthesis: entry tools ~£70/mo; independent all-rounders ~£300-1,200/mo; mid-market £400-1,500 (https://www.softomatesolutions.com/blog/estate-agency-software-uk-comparison/ — low-medium confidence). Alto/Street/SME Professional are quote-only.
- **The direct comparable, Kamma** (licensing compliance for agents): indicative **£100+VAT/mo for a 200-property self-serve portfolio = ~£0.50/property/mo**, CRM-integrated, with a done-for-you licence application service and an agent referral-revenue model (https://www.kammadata.com/how-we-can-help/letting-agents/). Medium confidence.
- Verdict on plausibility: a £49-99/mo add-on next to a £70-300 core system is **within budget norms** for an agent (unlike a landlord), but Kamma anchors agent compliance at ~£0.50/unit and offers more (licensing intelligence + fulfilment + revenue share). A flat £99 for a 60-property micro agent is £1.65/unit — 3x the specialist anchor.

## 4. Per-property vs flat, and the revenue model

Norms: per-unit prices cluster at **£1.00-£2.50/unit/mo** (LV extras £1.00-1.75; Alphaletz £1.40; Arthur ~£1.25-1.80; COHO £2.50 for a full ops platform; Kamma ~£0.50 for compliance-only). Minimums appear at £29.97-£62.50 where products replace real labour. Psychological breakpoints: sub-£10/mo consumer-app zone for small landlords; ~£30/mo where the tool is "the business system"; £50-100+ only for agents/Enterprise.

Modelling the three candidates against a realistic 4-50-property customer base (median ~10-12 properties, since most UK portfolio landlords sit at the bottom of that range):
- **Flat £49/mo → 48 customers**: price equals LetCompliance's *unlimited* top tier and 12x Landlord HQ's £49/yr. Every prospect who Googles alternatives finds a 90% cheaper substitute. Not winnable at volume.
- **Flat £99/mo → 24 customers**: equals Landlord Vision Enterprise (full suite, 25 tenancies). For a compliance-only tool this is DOA with landlords; with agents it is 2-3x the Kamma-implied rate for typical micro-agent book sizes.
- **£2/property, £29 min**: ARPU at the realistic median (10-15 props) is £29-30 — the minimum binds almost always → effectively a flat-£29 product needing **~78 customers**, worst of the three for revenue, and £29 still exceeds Hammock's entire MTD bookkeeping product for small portfolios. Only beats flat £49 above 25 properties, where buyers compare you to Kamma at £0.50/unit instead.
- **Revenue-maximising structure at target**: agent-flat. ~**30 agent offices × £79/mo = £2,370/mo** is the only arithmetic consistent with observed willingness to pay.

## 5. Billing and trial norms

- Annual discount is standard: Landlord Vision 2 months free on annual (promo), Alphaletz 2 months free, Hammock 20%, Lendlord £99/yr vs £12/mo, Landlord HQ £49/yr. Norm ≈ **15-20% off for annual**.
- Trials: Hammock 30 days, **no card required** (only card policy directly evidenced); Lendlord 30-day premium trial; free-forever tiers (Landlord Studio, Alphaletz, Lendlord, LetCompliance, Latch, Rentila) are the dominant acquisition pattern — landlords **expect to start free**, not card-first. Card-upfront trials are not evidenced anywhere in this market.

## Recommended price + packaging (if the concept proceeds at all)

**Do not sell compliance tracking to landlords at £49-99/mo — that segment's ceiling is ~£10-19/mo and its floor is £0.** The stated targets (24×£99 or 48×£49) require the agent segment:

1. **Primary SKU — letting agents: flat £79/mo per office, up to 150 managed properties** (then £0.50/unit — matching Kamma's rate rather than fighting it), positioned as the self-serve, no-CRM-integration-needed option for micro agents on 10Ninety/spreadsheets. Annual = 2 months free (£790/yr). 30-day trial, no card. Target: 30 offices = £2,370/mo.
2. **Optional defensive landlord SKU: £12/mo flat (or £120/yr) up to 15 properties** — at Lendlord-Premium parity, top of what evidence supports. Do not count on it for the revenue target (needs ~195 customers alone). No free tier unless forced — free tiers here are acquisition for suites with other monetisation, which a solo founder lacks.
3. Never per-property below ~25 units (the £29-minimum maths collapses to flat anyway), and never a £99 landlord price point.

**Evidence quality overall: medium.** No vendor page fetched directly (all proxy-blocked); prices triangulated from vendor-page search extracts (better) and competitor blogs (worse); Arthur and Landlord Studio UK GBP figures are the shakiest; no primary forum/survey data on budgets.

VERDICT: CAUTION — the stated pricing plan (24 customers at £99 or 48 at £49, landlord-direct) is a KILL on the evidence: the compliance-only job is already served at £0-£19/mo by at least seven live micro-tools, free tiers on the big suites, and NRLA's free member Portfolio, and £99/mo equals Landlord Vision's full Enterprise suite; the £2,335/mo target survives only if repackaged agent-first at ~£79/mo flat per office (~30 offices), where budgets support it but Kamma anchors compliance at ~£0.50/property/month.
