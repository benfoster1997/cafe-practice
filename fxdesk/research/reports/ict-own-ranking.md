# Which model does ICT himself rank best, and what has he taught since 2024?

Research date: 10 November 2026. Web search budget used: 12 of 12. Written for the FX desk project (forex majors, D1/H4/H1 context, M15 entry, held for hours, placed by hand as a pending order).

## How to read the source grades

Every factual claim carries a link and one of four grades.

- **PRIMARY** - ICT's own channel or an official document, read directly (YouTube's own page or feed, the World Cup Trading Championships site).
- **SECONDARY** - a transcript archive or third-party index that quotes him. Two caveats apply to every transcript quote in this report: the archives hold auto-generated captions (they contain mis-hearings), and I could only pull short fragments, so wording was not checked against the audio.
- **SECONDARY (AI summary)** - a machine-written summary of a video. Low reliability; used only where nothing better was reachable.
- **SNIPPET** - seen only in a search extract.
- **INFERENCE** - my own reasoning.

I could not read ICT's posts on X at all, and I could not pull transcripts for any 2025-2026 video directly from YouTube. That limits section 2 to titles, a transcript-based index site, and AI summaries. See the last section.

Two housekeeping notes. The third-party channel index (videohighlight.com) re-numbers its pages as new videos are added, so a "channel index" link may open a neighbouring page; the titles and dates are what matter. Dates given for the paid-era Core Content and Charter lectures (2016-2019) are approximate.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **ICT has never named one model as his best and stuck to it.** Across 2017-2025 he applied a superlative to at least six different things: One Shot One Kill ("my bread and butter"), the Universal model ("the core of which model I trade mostly"), the FVG + order block + OTE combination ("the gold standard"), the 2022 model ("an ATM machine"), the Silver Bullet ("you'll need to look at nothing further than this"), and Venom ("very, very powerful"). I found no statement of the form "if I could keep only one". (INFERENCE from the SECONDARY quotes in section 1.)
2. **His one consistent instruction is about method, not model:** pick one setup, learn it, journal and backtest it, and do not expect it to trade every day. (SECONDARY, section 1.)
3. **Since September 2024 almost everything he teaches is Nasdaq futures on 15-second to 5-minute charts around the 9:30 New York open.** Of the fifteen most recent uploads on his channel (6 Sep to 28 Oct 2026), ten name NQ futures in the title, the rest are news-day reviews and new-student lessons, and none names forex or a currency pair ([official channel feed](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCtjxa77NqamhVC8atV85Rog), PRIMARY).
4. **The models that fit this project best are his oldest, not his newest.** The forex day-trade models from about 2016-2019 (Charter Model 11, Charter Model 5, the London-open Judas swing) are the only ones he demonstrates on forex pairs with H1/M15 charts, 12-40 pip stops and 20-50 pip objectives. That is a fit judgement of mine (INFERENCE), and it is not his ranking.
5. **Nothing found in this round is evidence that any of these models is profitable.** His own record remains unverified (section 5).

**What this adds up to (INFERENCE).**

- *The one to learn first:* the nearest thing to a standing answer is the 2022 model. He ended that series saying "you have enough", later called it "excellent", and labelled its Charter restatement "foundational". The digest already recorded that he tells newcomers to start with the 41 videos. For his son in 2024 he built an even narrower version (Calub's model: one FVG entry, one trade a day).
- *The most reliable:* claimed for the Silver Bullet ("every single trading day"), with the escape clause that it may not appear in the market you are watching. That clause makes the claim untestable as stated.
- *The one he trades most:* "bread and butter" = One Shot One Kill (2017); "the core of which model I trade mostly" = Universal model (Charter era). Both are forex, both pre-2020.
- *The one he would keep if he could keep only one:* **not found.**
- *The Unicorn:* I found no statement from ICT ranking it, and no primary source for when he named it. In a transcript-based index of his 1 Nov 2023 Market Maker Models lecture, "unicorn" is the label for the second-stage re-accumulation leg of the buy model, not a stand-alone model ([x10hop](https://x10hop.pages.dev/concepts/market-maker-buy-sell-model-2023), SECONDARY). The popular "breaker + fair value gap overlap = Unicorn" template is attributed to him by vendors without a cited video or date ([LuxAlgo library](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/unicorn.md), SECONDARY). Treat the Unicorn-as-a-model as community packaging until a primary source is found.

## 6. Points a rule specification should carry forward

1. **Do not write "ICT's best model" into the spec.** There is no such thing on the record. Write "the ICT forex day-trade model family (2016-2019)" and tag the vintage. (INFERENCE)
2. **If the 2022 entry is used on M15, tag it as our adaptation.** His own basis for scaling is one sentence ("Everything Is fractal everything is scalable") and a list of timeframes in the Model 13 lecture ([transcript](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Klout9/ict-strat/master/Month%2011%20-%20Charter%20Price%20Action%20Models/34_Model_13_Charter_Lecture_2022_YouTube_Model.md), SECONDARY).
3. **Expect few signals.** His own frequency statements for the forex day-trade material: "try to look for one good setup per week" (Model 5); these trades "form several times a week" (Core Month 8); the Judas swing forms "a few times a week" and not every day; day trading "does not mean or equate to Everyday trading" (sources in sections 1 and 3, SECONDARY). An any-hour alert app built on this will be quiet most days.
4. **Day-of-week and session filters are part of the models, not optional extras.** Model 5 is Tue-Thu; Model 11 and the Core content favour Mon-Wed; the Core rules say skip New York if London has already made about 80% of the average daily range, and avoid FOMC and payrolls days ([Essentials transcript](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Klout9/ict-strat/master/Month%2008%20-%20Daytrading/01_Essentials_To_ICT_Daytrading.md), SECONDARY).
5. **The session he calls easiest is contradictory across eras:** New York open in the 2016 Core content, the London Judas swing in the primer video. Test both; assume neither. (SECONDARY sources in section 1; the recommendation is INFERENCE.)
6. **Daily bias remains the hole.** Nothing taught since August 2024 turns bias into a rule; the newer material makes it more narrative-driven. Any bias rule will again be our own proxy and must be tagged as such. (INFERENCE from section 2.3.)
7. **The 2025-26 additions are optional filters at most.** The "loaded deal" idea (two arrays overlapping, reached at a session open or macro time, arrays up to 60 days old, skip when arrays conflict) is the only post-2024 concept that transfers to forex D1/H4/H1 context without the 9:30 open. It has no independent test. (SECONDARY for the concept; INFERENCE for the transfer.)
